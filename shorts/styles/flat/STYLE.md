# Style: flat (default, modelled on the owner's sample video)

**Use for:** any topic. This is the channel's default look. Use the other styles only when the owner asks.

**Look:** flat vector explainer on a dark navy background (#151C2B), with two soft red and blue glows that drift slowly. Objects are simple shapes with soft drop shadows:
- dark round medallions holding an icon
- cream stars
- dashed "?" slots that fill in one by one
- a corkboard with photo cards, a red pin and string, and red X marks
- a top-down map with blocks scrolling past a hero icon

Orange (`accent`) marks the key object or number. Blue (`accent2`) is only the caption pill.

**Captions:** small (about 66 px), sentence case, near the top (y about 360). One phrase of up to 4 words at a time. Words light up as they are spoken; upcoming words are faint. Words in `highlight` get a blue pill when spoken.

**Music:** light major-key plucks, 96 bpm, ducked under the voice.

**Voice:** `en-US-AndrewNeural`, rate +6%: friendly explainer.

**Shot mix (8–13 shots):**
- Open with `question` (big orange ? and a 2–3 word teaser), then cut by 1.5 s.
- Show "who / which ones" with `board` (2–3 cards, `x: true` to cross one out).
- Show levels, scores or "how many out of N" with `rating`.
- Show a list of things being collected or revealed with `slots`.
- Use `map` with hero + followers for movement: travel, chases, migration, spread.
- Use `number` for one big stat, `vs` for this-or-that, and `emoji` (medallion) for a single object.
- End with a shot that mirrors the first (same `question`) so the loop is seamless.

**Avoid:**
- emoji inside text fields (they render as boxes; QA warns)
- more than 4 words in labels
- two `question` shots in a row
- copying the sample's game-specific art or script
