# Style: cosmos

**Use for:** space, planets, stars, black holes, light, time, physics, the scale of the universe.

**Look:** deep navy-to-violet gradient, slowly drifting twinkling starfield, soft nebula glow. Text is white, highlights are gold (`accent`) and secondary marks are cyan (`accent2`).

**Music:** slow minor-key ambient pad with soft arpeggio, 84 bpm. Ducked under the voice.

**Voice:** `en-US-AndrewNeural`, rate +6%: calm, awed.

**Shot mix:**
- Open with `question` or `headline` + a space emoji (🌌 🪐 🌙 ☀️ 🌍 ⭐ 🚀 🔭 🕳️).
- Use `number` for distances, ages and temperatures; `orbit` for anything that circles or cycles; `vs` for size comparisons.
- Close with an `orbit` or `headline` that mirrors the opening.

**Highlight:** big numbers, units, and the one "wow" word per sentence.

**Avoid:** more than 2 `headline` shots in a row, and red tones.
