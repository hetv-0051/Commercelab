# Style: archive

**Use for:** history, ancient civilisations, archaeology, inventions, famous mysteries, wars, old customs.

**Look:** sepia parchment with film grain, dust motes and vignette, in a serif display font. Text is cream, highlights are crimson (`accent`) and secondary marks are gold (`accent2`).

**Music:** slow, low minor pad with soft pulse, 72 bpm. Mysterious and documentary-like.

**Voice:** `en-GB-RyanNeural`, rate +4%: storyteller.

**Shot mix:**
- Open with `question` or `headline` with a period emoji (🏛️ 📜 ⚔️ 👑 🏺 🗿 ⏳ 🗺️ 🏰).
- Every history video should have at least one `timeline` with real years.
- Use `number` for "years ago" and counts, and `vs` for then vs now.

**Highlight:** years, names, and the twist word.

**Avoid:** modern tech emoji unless the line is about "now". Use dates only if they're verified.
