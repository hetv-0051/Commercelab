# Style: alert

**Use for:** survival, disasters, "what if" scenarios, extreme limits of the body, dangerous animals, close calls.

**Look:** near-black with red pulse, scanlines and a moving sweep. Text is white, highlights are amber (`accent`) and secondary marks are red (`accent2`).

**Music:** tense minor pulse with ticking hats, 122 bpm.

**Voice:** `en-US-AndrewNeural`, rate +8%: urgent but controlled.

**Shot mix:**
- Open with `question` ("What if…?") using a giant `?` or ⚠️.
- Use `number` for countdowns and limits ("3 minutes without air"), `bars` for survival-time comparisons, and `timeline` for a minute-by-minute scenario (the `year` field can hold "0 min", "3 min"…).
- End on a `question` or `headline` that loops back.

**Highlight:** time limits, numbers, and the danger word.

**Avoid:** fear-mongering and unverified survival advice. Frame as science, not instructions.
