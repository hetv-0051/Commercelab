# GTA 6 topic bank

The channel niche is **GTA 6 facts, features and explanations**, told in the flat style.

Each run:
- **Prefer fresh news.** Search the web first: launch sales, patches, records, discoveries players are sharing, Rockstar announcements, GTA Online news.
- **Fall back to this bank** only when nothing fresh beats it.
- **Re-verify every fact** against a reliable source before use: Rockstar Newswire, Take-Two investor releases, IGN, GameSpot, Eurogamer, VGC, Polygon, The Verge, major reviews.
- **Never repeat** a topic already in `_published.md`.
- **Never copy** another creator's script. Your own words only.
